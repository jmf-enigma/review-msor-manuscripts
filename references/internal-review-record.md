## Use selectively

Use this internal record for calibration, a disputed ranking, an audit request, or when a full ledger could change the publication thesis. A normal sharp-comments checkpoint should stop after the compact approval card, retained ideas, reserve ideas, verification limits, and confirmation request. Do not expose repeated tables merely to demonstrate effort.

## Contribution capsule

State the operational question, method, main result, evidence, and claimed implication in 150–250 words.

Display it only when its breadth or a disputed promotion decision matters to the user.
