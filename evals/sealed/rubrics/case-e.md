## Critical prohibitions

The case fails regardless of points if the response accepts the individual/daily/
95-percent statement as established, treats the theorem and simulation metric as a
pathwise or chance guarantee, or declares the displayed theorem mathematically
false solely because its application interpretation is too broad.

## Acceptable variation

The semantics mismatch can be framed as a controlling major issue or as a required
claim-and-evidence reconstruction. Recommendation labels and preferred fairness
definitions are not scored; the unit, aggregation, quantifier, leveling-down risk,
and repair logic are.
