derivative, declares the switching-surface theorem invalid because of this
independent corollary, or omits any meaningful Motivation--Execution--Insight
assessment after locating the error.

minor correction depending on the response's journal-threshold logic. Its local
reach and the surviving contribution must remain explicit. Exact recommendation
